1. Introduction

HEAD OF
DEPARTMENT
You prepare your department's activity memos, keep its workplan and budget, add your officers to the staff list, and report on each activity after it ends.

1.1 What OCEO is for

OCEO is the system the Office of the CEO uses to plan, approve and follow up the Board's field activities. Every activity that takes officers out of the office starts as an activity memo. The memo says what the activity is, where and when it happens, who goes and what it costs. It is reviewed, decided by the CEO, carried out, and closed with a report.

Before OCEO, these memos moved on paper and by email. Now each memo has one record that shows who did what and when, its budget is checked against your approved allocation, and the officers on it are checked so nobody is sent to two places on the same day.

1.2 What you can and cannot see

OCEO is confidential. As a Head of Department you see your own department's work only.

Warning: Never share your password or let someone else use your account. Everything you open, print or download is recorded in the access log, under your name.

2.3 Rules that always apply

  • Every memo is reviewed by the Assistant Chief of Staff or the Chief of Staff before it reaches the CEO.
  • Nothing is deleted after you submit. A draft can be discarded. A submitted memo can only be returned, declined, postponed or cancelled, and its history stays.
  • Only an approved budget counts. A memo is charged to a line in your department's approved budget.
  • An officer cannot be in two places at once. Officers on an approved activity cannot be put on another one on the same days.
  • Comments are kept as written. Review comments and the CEO's reasons cannot be edited or removed.

3. Getting Started

3.1 Your first sign-in

When you are appointed Head of Department, OCEO emails you a link to set your own password. The email has the subject OCEO: set your password and goes to your email address on the staff list.

  1. Open the email and press the button in it. The link expires after a while, so use it soon.
  2. Type a new password twice and press Reset password. Choose one you do not use anywhere else.
  3. On the sign-in page, type your email and new password and press Sign in.
  4. The first time only, read the NG-CDF Board Data Privacy Statement, tick to agree and accept it.
Tip: No email? Look in your spam or junk folder first. If it is not there, ask the System Administrator to check your email on the staff list and press Email password link on your account to send a new one.

3.2 Two-factor sign-in

OCEO may ask you to protect your account with a second step. Install an authenticator app on your phone (for example Google Authenticator or Microsoft Authenticator), scan the code OCEO shows, and type the six-digit number from the app. Keep the recovery codes OCEO gives you somewhere safe. From then on, OCEO asks for the number from your phone each time you sign in.

3.3 Forgotten password or locked account

  1. On the sign-in page press Forgot password?
  2. Type your work email and press Send reset link.
  3. Open the email, set a new password and sign in.

The page gives the same answer whatever email you type, so nobody can use it to find out who has an account. After several wrong passwords your account is locked for a while. Resetting your password unlocks it at once.

3.4 Finding your way

The menu on the left shows only what you use:

MenuWhat you do there

Staff listFind and add the officers of your department.

Press your name at the top right to see your profile, change your password, manage two-factor sign-in, or Sign out. Always sign out when you leave your computer.

3.5 Alerts, emails and SMS

The bell at the top of every page lists your alerts, for example when a memo is returned to you, approved or declined. While OCEO is open, a small pop-up also appears as each new alert arrives. Press an alert to go straight to the memo.

When something needs you to act, you also receive an email and, if your mobile number is on the staff list, an SMS. For confidentiality these give only the memo reference and the step. Sign in to see the details, and do not forward them.

4. Your Staff List

Only officers on the staff list can be put on a team. Human Resources loads most of the list. If an officer of your department is missing, you can add them.

4.1 Adding an officer

  1. Open Staff list and search for the officer by name or PF number, to be sure they are not already there.
  2. Press Add officer.
  3. Fill in the name, PF number, designation, employment type, job grade, official email and mobile number.
  4. Press Save.
Important: The job grade sets the officer's DSA rate, so take it from HR's records. Casual staff are on scale 10. Attachees without a grade are on INTERN. Type mobile numbers as Kenyan numbers, for example 0712 345 678.

4.2 Importing a list

  1. On Staff list press Import and choose your filled-in staff file.
  2. Press Check file. OCEO shows how many officers would be added and lists any row with a problem.
  3. Correct the file if needed, then import it.

4.3 What you cannot change

You can correct the officers you added yourself. You cannot deactivate an officer, or change anyone's department, designation or system access. When an officer leaves, transfers or is promoted, inform Human Resources or the System Administrator, who record the change with its date.

5. Your Workplan and Budget

Each financial year (July to June) your department prepares a workplan with its budget estimates. When the CEO approves it, it becomes your department's budget, and your memos are charged to its lines.

5.1 Preparing the workplan

  1. Open Workplan and budget and choose the financial year at the top.
  2. To prepare it in Excel, press Workplan template, fill it in and save it.
  3. Press Prepare or import the workplan. Choose your file under Workplan in Excel, or leave it empty to type the lines in.
  4. For each line, check the objective, deliverables, quantity and unit cost, quarters and responsible officer.
  5. Fill in the Objectives for the year. You may attach the workplan document too.
Tip: If the import finds a mistake, such as a quarter written as Q5, nothing is imported and the message names the row. Correct the file and import it again.

5.2 Submitting it to the CEO

Press Submit to the CEO and confirm. The lines are then locked while the CEO reviews them. The budget does not count until it is approved.

5.3 When the CEO returns it

The CEO may comment on the workplan or on a line, change a line's amount, or return it to you. You are alerted, and the badge on Workplan and budget shows it.

  1. Open the workplan and read the orange box and the comments on the lines.
  2. Make the changes asked for.
  3. Press Resubmit to the CEO.

Your proposed amounts stay visible beside any the CEO changed, and every step is kept in the Review history.

5.4 After approval

Once approved, the lines no longer change. If you need more on a line, or to move money between lines, ask the Office of the CEO: they handle budget adjustments, and the CEO approves them.

6.1 Before you start

7. Adding the Team and Costs

On the saved memo, press Team and costs.

7.1 Adding officers

  1. Press Add officers.
  2. Search by name, or filter by department and press Select all shown.
  3. Tick the officers, choose their role (for example Team lead or Member) and press Add to team.

OCEO adds each officer's DSA automatically from their job grade and the number of nights.

7.2 External participants and groups

  • For a person from outside the Board, press Add external participant.
  • For many people you do not need to name, such as 40 youth at a forum, press Add a group by number, give the group a name and the headcount, and choose one DSA scale.

7.3 Cost lines

Press Add cost line for anything other than DSA: air tickets, bus fare, fuel, vehicle hire, venue, materials. Choose the cost type, describe it and type the amount. If a ticket is for one officer, choose that officer.

7.4 How DSA is worked out

  • There is one daily rate per job grade, wherever in Kenya the activity takes place.
  • An activity of two days or more pays the night before the start (for travel) and every night up to the end: the number of days plus one.
  • A one-day activity pays no nights.
ExampleNights paidScale 6 officerCasual (scale 10)

Rates shown are those in force from 1 July 2026. OCEO always uses the current rate.

7.5 When an officer is not available

An officer already on an approved activity on any of the same days cannot be added, and OCEO tells you why. An officer on a memo still waiting for a decision shows a warning, but can be added. Only the Office of the CEO can override this, with a reason. If you need an officer who is booked, speak to the Office of the CEO.

8. Checking and Submitting

8.1 Previewing the memo

Press Preview memo. OCEO builds the memo in the Board's template: TO, FROM, DATE, REF NO and SUBJECT; your sections; Annex A, the Activity Budget Request; and the list of participants. Read it as the CEO will. The total must agree everywhere and the budget line must be named.

If anything required is missing, OCEO lists it and will not let you submit until it is fixed.

8.2 Submitting for review

  1. Press Submit for review.
  2. Choose who reviews it: the Assistant Chief of Staff or the Chief of Staff.
  3. Confirm. The memo moves to the Awaiting review and decision tab and is locked.
Warning: Check the memo carefully first. A draft can be thrown away with Discard draft, but a submitted memo is never deleted.

8.3 Signing as acting Head

If you are acting in the role, tick I am signing in an acting capacity when you submit. The memo then reads "Ag." before your designation, for example "Ag. Manager, ICT". Your record on the staff list does not change.

9. When a Memo Is Returned

A reviewer or the CEO may return your memo for correction. You get an alert and an email, and the memo appears on the Returned for correction tab.

  1. Open the memo and read the comment at the top. It says what must change.
  2. Press Edit memo, or open Team and costs, and make the changes.
  3. Press Preview memo to check it again.
  4. Press Resubmit for review. It goes back to the same office.
Tip: If a comment is not clear, call the reviewer before you change the memo. A second return costs more time than a short call.

10. After the CEO Decides

10.1 When the memo is approved

You are alerted and the memo moves to Approved and authorised. On approval the CEO instructs Finance to process the imprest and Supply Chain to procure the tickets, and may instruct other departments, for example Administration for a vehicle. You are told when each instruction is done, with its reference, such as the imprest or LPO number.

From the start date the memo shows In progress, and after the end date Completed. Your report is then due.

10.2 When the memo is declined

The memo moves to the Declined tab with the CEO's reason. A declined memo is final. If the activity is still needed, prepare a new memo that answers the reason.

10.3 Changes after approval

If the dates, the team or the costs must change after approval, speak to the Office of the CEO first. Larger changes go back to the CEO for a new decision. If an officer is needed elsewhere, only the Office of the CEO can recall or release them.

11. Reporting After the Activity

11.1 When the report is due

The report is due 7 working days after the end date. OCEO records whether it came in on time, from the date it was first received.

11.2 Submitting the report

  1. Open the memo and press Submit activity report.
  2. Write What the activity achieved, the Key findings and your Recommendations.
  3. Attach the report document.
  4. Under each cost line, type the amount actually spent. Type 0 for anything not used, such as DSA for an officer who did not travel.
  5. Press Submit report.
Important: The amounts you enter count as spent against your budget straight away, so take them from Finance's records.

11.3 If the report is returned

Press Correct and resubmit the report, make the change asked for and submit it again. When the Office of the CEO accepts the report, the activity is closed and nothing on it can change.

12. Asking an Officer to Prepare a Memo

You can ask an officer on your staff list to draft a memo for you. They do not need an account. The memo stays yours: you check it and decide whether it goes for review.

12.1 Sending the request

  1. On Activity memos press Ask an officer to prepare a memo.
  2. Choose the officer and say what the memo is for.
  3. Choose how many days the link works, up to 30, and press Send the link.

12.2 What the officer does

The officer opens the link and asks for a six-digit code, which is sent to their email or mobile on the staff list. With the code they can open that one memo only. They fill it in, add the team and costs, and send it to you.

12.3 Checking what they send

Open it from the Prepared by officers tab. Then either:

  • submit it for review as in Section 8, when it is ready;
  • press Return to the officer with a comment, and they receive a new link to correct it; or
  • press Decline if the memo is not needed.

While the officer has the memo, you cannot edit it. To stop a link at once, press Revoke on the memo requests list.

13. When the CEO Instructs Your Department

When the CEO approves a memo, the CEO may instruct your department to act on it: Finance to process the imprest, Supply Chain to procure the tickets, or any department with a written instruction. You then see Instructions in the menu, with a count of those still open.

  1. Open the instruction. It shows the reference, dates, places, team and the CEO's note, and for Finance and Supply Chain the cost lines you act on.
  2. Press Acknowledge so the Office of the CEO knows you have it.
  3. When it is done, press Mark as done and type the reference, such as the imprest or LPO number.

The Office of the CEO and the memo's department are told at once. Once the memo is approved, you can also read it and its documents, but not change anything on it.

# CLAUDE.md

Guidance for Claude Code in `activities.ngcdf.go.ke`, **NG-CDF FAPM**: the CEO's confidential Field Activity Planning and Monitoring system.

## Read first

- `docs/FRD-TRACEABILITY.md`: every FRD requirement and where it lives. The FRD (v1.2, RESTRICTED) is the specification; `docs/DESIGN-ASSESSMENT.md` is the earlier design and is overridden by the FRD where they differ.
- UI follows Smart's PAKA-RANGI standard (`../smart.ngcdf.go.ke/documentation/standards/PAKA-RANGI.md`). Use the components in `resources/views/components/ui/` and the class recipes in `App\Support\Ui`; do not hand-roll buttons, popups or page shells. No `wire:confirm` (use a confirm popup), no per-page `<style>`.

## Database safety — absolute

Never run `migrate:fresh`, `migrate:refresh`, `db:wipe`, `migrate:rollback` against a real database, or any `DROP`/`TRUNCATE`/unscoped `DELETE`, for any reason. Tests use SQLite `:memory:` (`phpunit.xml`). Schema changes are new, additive migrations. If a migration fails partway, stop and report the state; do not clean up by dropping anything. (Same rule as Smart NG-CDF, after the 2026-08-05 data loss there.)

## Confidentiality rules (FRD 7.12) — keep them true

- Exactly five roles: `ceo`, `chief_of_staff`, `assistant_chief_of_staff` (FRD §4.1); `admin`, the System Administrator, approved by the CEO on 11 October 2026 for settings, configuration and the access and audit log only; and `hod`, a Head of Department (activity memo workflow, October 2026; record the CEO's approval in the next FRD revision), one active account per department. Neither `admin` nor `hod` has `activities.view`: `admin` must never reach activity data, dashboards or any report but `access-audit`; `hod` reaches only their own department's memos (`Activity::scopeVisibleTo` / `isVisibleTo`, the `memos.*` routes and `DocumentController`), prepares their own department's workplan and budget (`workplans.prepare`), asks officers to prepare memos through delegated links, builds their team and costs on `Memos\Team` (overlaps flagged, never naming the other activity; no field-day totals), and sees the staff list with `staff.add` (adds officers one by one or by importing a list, corrects only those they added, never deactivates; `App\Services\StaffRecords`, `App\Services\StaffImporter`), and their own department's budget position (`BudgetPosition::forDepartment` / `forActivity`: allocation, spent, committed, pending, balance; on the memos board, memo form and memo page), never another department's, the register, dashboards, calendar or reports. Permissions are `RolesAndPermissionsSeeder::MATRIX`. Never add another role or a way to self-register.
- Every service method that changes data checks the user's permission itself; route middleware is not enough.
- Every view of an activity, export, print and download goes through `App\Services\AccessLogger`. Model changes are audited by the `Auditable` trait.
- Exports carry the RESTRICTED marking, the user and the time (`App\Exports\ReportExport`, `resources/views/reports/document.blade.php`).
- Files are written and read only through `App\Services\DocumentStore` (encrypted at rest).
- Nothing is permanently deleted: drafts are soft-deleted, everything else is cancelled; lists are deactivated or merged.
- No outbound integration, and in-app alerts only to the Office of the CEO and, about their own department's memos, its Head of Department (`App\Services\Alerts`; FRD 12, BR-11). One exception (25 Oct 2026): the Head of a department the CEO instructs receives the instruction, below.
- Email and SMS (25 Oct 2026; record the CEO's approval in the next FRD revision): only an alert marked `ActivityAlert::needsAction()` also goes out, through `App\Services\OutboundAlerts` only, to the same recipients when they are the CEO (since 25 Oct 2026, at the CEO's request: memos awaiting decision, budgets awaiting approval, reports, reminders), a Head of Department, the Chief of Staff or the Assistant. Messages carry the reference and the step only: never the title, places, team, costs, comments or names of officers. SMS go through SMS Leopard (`App\Contracts\SmsGateway`, `SMSLEOPARD_*`, as in NG-CDF Careers; the log driver only in tests). Every message is logged in `outbound_messages` (report `message-log`); a failed delivery is marked failed and never undoes the action. Settings › Thresholds `alerts_email` / `alerts_sms` switch each channel. The SMS number is the account's `phone`, else its linked officer's (`User::mobileNumber`); accounts are made from the staff list (`staff_id`), and staff phones are Kenyan mobiles stored as +2547XXXXXXXX (`App\Support\MobileNumber`).
- Instructions to departments (25 Oct 2026, any department 26 Oct 2026; record the CEO's approval in the next FRD revision): when approving, or later on an approved activity, only the CEO (`instructions.send`) instructs the departments that must act, through `App\Services\ActionInstructions` only: Finance to process the imprest and Procurement to procure the travel tickets (one of each per activity; departments set in Settings › Thresholds, default Financial Services - Accounts and Supply Chain), and any other department with a written instruction (`InstructionKind::Other`, one per department, no cost lines shown). Instructions are validated before the approval is recorded. That department's Head (`instructions.action`, `ActionInstruction::scopeFor` / `isFor`) sees an instruction sheet only: reference, dates, places, team, the CEO's note and the cost lines they act on (Procurement: travel and vehicle hire). The sheet never shows the memo's justification, reviews, documents, budget position or DSA lines for Procurement. Once approved, the Head of an instructed department may also read the approved memo, its PDF and documents, read only (`Activity::isReadableByInstructedHead` / `isReadableBy`, 25 Oct 2026, on the CEO's request; no budget position, team page, report or actions); `Activity::isVisibleTo` is unchanged. The Office of the CEO (`activities.view`) can open every instruction sheet without acting on it. They acknowledge, then complete with a reference, which alerts the Office of the CEO and the memo's department.

## Domain rules

- `Activity::status` changes only through `App\Services\ActivityLifecycle`, which checks `ActivityStatus::allowedTransitions()`, locks the row and re-checks the status (so a double click or two users cannot apply a step twice), and writes the status history.
- Activity memo workflow: a Head of Department's memo goes Draft → Under review (with `review_office` ACOS or COS) → Awaiting decision; a reviewer can return it (Returned for correction, reason required) and it is resubmitted to the same office. `forward()` sends it on, via the Chief of Staff when Settings › Thresholds `acos_forwards_via_cos` is Yes and the Assistant forwards. Forwarding never approves. The Office of the CEO can prepare a memo on a Head of Department's behalf (`responsible_hod_id` / `responsible_hod_name`; `created_by` is the real preparer). Review comments are `ActivityReviewNote`, append-only. Board tabs, counts and the memo report come from `App\Services\MemoTracker`. User guides: `docs/guides/FAPM-Activity-Memo-Workflow-Guide.pdf`, and the 3-page `docs/guides/FAPM-Activity-Memo-Quick-Guide.pdf` (generated memo, availability, budget position).
- Memo routing and signing (25 Oct 2026; route confirmed 26 Oct 2026): a memo always goes Head of Department → Assistant or Chief of Staff (review) → CEO (decision); there is no direct route to the CEO, and the Assistant's memos pass the Chief of Staff whenever `acos_forwards_via_cos` is Yes. After approving, the CEO instructs the departments that must act (see Instructions below). The Head signs with `User::signingDesignation()` (staff-list designation, department), kept on the memo with the acting choice (`activities.hod_designation`, `hod_acting`; "Ag." before the designation only; the officer's record never changes). A Head's memo needs `source_reference` (REF NO) and `issued_on` (DATE): validated on the form and blocking in `MemoDocument::issues` (not for an officer preparing it); Office-recorded memos keep `source_received_on`. The memo PDF follows `files/MEMO Template .docx` (US Letter; `resources/views/memos/document.blade.php`, `memos/partials/signatures`).
- Delegated memos (25 Oct 2026; record the CEO's approval in the next FRD revision): a Head of Department asks an officer on the staff list to prepare one memo (`App\Services\DelegatedMemos`, a `delegated` `SubmissionLink`, not behind `ACTIVITIES_SUBMISSION_LINKS`). The link is hashed, time-limited (≤ 30 days), opens only after a six-digit code sent to the officer's staff-list email or mobile (10 minutes, 5 tries, 3 codes per 10 minutes, session trusted 2 hours), and opens that memo only (`App\Livewire\MemoRequest`). The officer never gets an account: edits go through `ActivityEditor::asPreparer()` under the issuing Head's rules for that memo, audited under the officer's name (`AuditLogger::actingAs`). The memo is the Head's (`created_by`, `responsible_hod_id`) with `prepared_by_staff_id`. Draft → With the Head of Department (`with_hod`) → Returned to the preparer (new link) / Declined (by the Head; hidden from the Office of the CEO's declined tab) / Under review with the Chief of Staff, after which the existing workflow is unchanged. The Head cannot edit while the officer has it (`Activity::isWithPreparer`).
- Staff position and access changes (25 Oct 2026): only through `App\Services\StaffChanges` (`staff.reassign`: System Administrator, CEO, Chief of Staff, Assistant; office roles also need `users.manage`; nobody changes their own access). HR position (department, designation) and FAPM access (role) stay separate. One active Head per department (replace only when ticked); a Head moved to another department is made Head there or loses access; accounts are deactivated, never deleted, and their sessions end. Every change is an immutable `StaffChange` with effective date and reason. Accounts are made from the staff list (`users.staff_id`).
- Availability (21 Oct 2026): an officer on an activity approved or later is unavailable on their own days; a Head of Department cannot override, the Office of the CEO only with a reason. Pending memos warn, drafts/declined/cancelled hold nobody. `App\Services\ParticipantChecks` (checked under a staff-row lock when adding and at approval). Recall/release/reassignment goes only through `App\Services\ParticipantReleases` (Office of the CEO; only computed DSA reduces automatically; never deletes the assignment).
- The memo PDF and annexes come only from `App\Services\MemoDocument` (one source for every figure); its `blockingIssues()` gate a Head of Department's submission, and the generated memo replaces the signed upload for department-originated memos.
- Budgeting (21 Oct 2026): department budgets, their lines and adjustments change only through `App\Services\Budgeting`. The CEO, Chief of Staff or Assistant prepares a draft (`budgets.prepare`); the Chief of Staff or Assistant submits it to the CEO; only the CEO approves or returns it for correction with a reason (`budgets.approve`); the CEO may approve a draft the CEO prepared directly. An approved budget changes only by adjustments (supplementary, reduction, reallocation; a line never below zero), with the same approval. Only approved budgets count; only their lines can be charged by a memo. Menu: Budgeting › Department budgets, Budget adjustments, Budget position. Not in Reference data, and not for `admin`. Workplans (25 Oct 2026; record the CEO's approval in the next FRD revision): a Head of Department prepares their own department's workplan and budget estimates the same way (`workplans.prepare`, `Budgeting::canPrepareFor`): objectives, lines that are workplan activities (objective, deliverables, quantity × unit cost, quarters, responsible officer), typed in or imported from `App\Exports\WorkplanTemplate` (`App\Services\WorkplanImporter`; ICT example in `docs/guides`), and an optional encrypted workplan document. While submitted, the CEO comments on it or a line and changes line amounts (`reviseLine`; `budget_lines.proposed_amount` keeps the department's figure). Every submission, comment, change, return and approval is an immutable `BudgetReview` (with line snapshots). After approval `budget_lines.amount` never changes: the CEO adjusts directly (`adjustNow`, effective date, confirm step), the Office of the CEO proposes; each approved adjustment records the line allocation before and after (report `budget-adjustments`). Current = approved + increases − reductions.
- Activity report (21 Oct 2026): `activities:daily` starts approved activities on their start date and completes them after the end date; the report is due 7 working days later (`report_due_working_days`). The Head of Department submits it on the memo page with the actual amount spent on every cost line (counts as spent at once); the COS, ACOS or CEO accepts or returns it (`ActivityLifecycle::submitReport` / `acceptReport` / `returnReport`). On time or late is measured from the date first received (`Activity::reportTimeliness`, report `report-compliance`). No overdue reminder goes to the department (RP-03).
- Budget position figures come only from `App\Services\BudgetPosition` (allocation = approved budgets with approved adjustments; balance = allocation − spent − committed; pending never deducted; spent only from Finance's actual amounts).
- Groups by number (25 Oct 2026): `ActivityEditor::addGroup` adds officers without names (`activity_participants.group_label`, `headcount` 1 to 500, one DSA scale; no `staff_id`). DSA = rate × nights × headcount; `staff_count`, the memo annex and analytics count `people()`. Groups are never availability-checked, field-day limited, recalled or released (name-based checks filter `whereNotNull('staff_id')`).
- Details, locations, team and planned costs change through `App\Services\ActivityEditor`; after submission these become amendments and may return the activity to the CEO (BR-10).
- Stored totals on `activities` are maintained by `App\Services\ActivityCosting::refresh()`; DSA lines by `App\Services\DsaCalculator`.
- DSA (22 Oct 2026): one standard rate per job grade, whatever the destination (`DsaRate::STANDARD`). Job grades are the scales in the HR staff register (Job Scale 1 to 10, stored as the bare number, e.g. `6`, since 24 Oct 2026; `DsaRate::normaliseGrade` also reads `NGCDF 6`, `Scale 6`, `Job Group 6`), and INTERN at KES 4,000 for casuals and attachees without a grade), from 1 Jul 2026, synced by `SettingsSeeder` on every run. `DsaRate::lookup` reads only rates with no destination; the retired Cities / Other towns rows are history only. A county location only decides whether DSA applies. Completed, closed, declined and cancelled activities are never recalculated. Job grades are validated by `App\Rules\DsaGrade` / `DsaRate::normaliseGrade`. Payable nights (25 Oct 2026) come only from `App\Support\DsaPeriod`: two days or more pay the departure night before the start and every night to the end date (days + 1); one day pays none; the "days" basis pays per day. Every casual is on scale 10 (KES 6,300, `EmploymentType::defaultDsaGrade`); attachees on INTERN unless graded. `activities:refresh-pending-dsa` recalculates memos not yet approved; production casual update: `docs/deploy/2026-10-25-casual-staff-dsa-scale-10.sql` (run by hand).
- Generated documents (memo PDF and annexes, report PDF and print, Excel exports) use Gill Sans MT through `App\Support\DocumentFont`. The font is licensed with Microsoft Office, so its files are never committed: they go in `storage/app/fonts` (or `ACTIVITIES_DOCUMENT_FONTS`) on each server; without them PDFs fall back to DejaVu Sans.
- Money is integer cents in PHP (`App\Support\Money`) and DECIMAL(15,2) in the database. No float arithmetic (BR-09).
- Financial year is July to June (`App\Support\FinancialYear`). Dates display as `d M Y`.
- Configurable thresholds live in `App\Services\AppSettings` (editable in Settings › Thresholds).
- Reports are classes in `app/Reports/Definitions`, registered in `ReportCatalog`; one definition drives screen, Excel, PDF and print.
- Magic-link intake (`App\Livewire\Submit`, `ActivitySubmission`) is behind `ACTIVITIES_SUBMISSION_LINKS`, off by default, pending the CEO's confirmation. A submission is always a Draft.

## Commands

```bash
composer install && npm install && npm run build
php artisan migrate && php artisan db:seed          # see DatabaseSeeder: system data synced every run; editable config seeded once, never overwritten
php artisan activities:create-user ceo you@ngcdf.go.ke "Full Name"   # also chief_of_staff, assistant_chief_of_staff, admin; hod needs --department="ICT"
composer run dev                                      # serve + queue + logs + vite
php artisan test --compact                            # Pest, SQLite :memory:
vendor/bin/pint --dirty --format agent                # after changing PHP
php artisan schedule:work                             # locally: daily checks and Monday summary
```
